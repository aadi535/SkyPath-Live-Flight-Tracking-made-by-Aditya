About the data

Live observations come from OpenSky Network. Coverage varies, and positions may be delayed. The map shows up to 300 aircraft and checks for updates about every five minutes.

Sample flights are invented. They appear while the live feed loads or when it is unavailable. Registration country is not the departure location. SkyPath is for exploration, not navigation.

Built by Aditya Raj (Suryavanshi) · Flask, Leaflet, and OpenStreetMap.
